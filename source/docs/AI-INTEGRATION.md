# Adaptive Guides

Six original Guide identities use the project's local branching coaching engine. Questions respond to saved choices, concerns and earned levels. Guide conversations save to private Firestore records; the player can keep a selected conversation in the Proof Bank.

No OpenAI, other model API, runtime image generation or secret AI credential is used. The generated art is a bundled development asset. Boss rounds evaluate explicit strategy choices; reflections are saved, not semantically graded by a language model. Nine worlds and 27 boss rounds are currently authored. New worlds require future authored content and corresponding progression/rule updates.
